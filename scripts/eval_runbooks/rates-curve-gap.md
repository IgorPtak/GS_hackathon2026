# Yield curve has a gap

Hi all! This is what we do when the curve is missing a tenor (usualy 10Y). Sorry its not pretty.

1. Open the curves dashboard and look which tenor is missing.
2. Open a ticket for the rates team with the missing tenor in the summary.
3. If still missing after 15 minutes page the team lead (escalate). Only do this when you are really sure!
